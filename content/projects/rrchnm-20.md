Network visualization designed for the 20th anniversary of the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media. It highlighted the relationship between people and projects at RRCHNM. This network visualization sought to answer the question: How are the people and projects of Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media related? How can we visualize these relationships over a twenty-year history?
