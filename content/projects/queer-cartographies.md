In collaboration with [Annelise Heinz](https://www.amheinz.org/), [Eric Gonzaba](https://ericnolangonzaba.net/), and [Cameron Blevins](https://cblevins.github.io), our project _Expanding Queer Cartographies_ maps queer spaces and communities in the late 20th century. We address a critical gap in LGBTQ+ spatial history: where were the women? While gay men’s venues are increasingly documented, we know much less about where lesbian women gathered. Our project transcribes and maps tens of thousands of listings of women- and lesbian-friendly venues printed in _Gaia’s Guide_, the premier lesbian travel guide of the 1970s-1990s.
