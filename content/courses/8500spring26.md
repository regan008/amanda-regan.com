{{< policy title="Artificial intelligence (AI) use" >}}
All students in the Digital History Ph.D. program are bound by the program's AI Use Policy (handbook, p. 10); this course aligns with it. **In Weeks 1–9, AI tools are strictly prohibited** — you must develop foundational computational-thinking skills without AI assistance. Any use before the instructor grants permission results in automatic failure of the assignment and is reported as an academic-integrity violation.

Beginning Week 10, the instructor introduces guardrails. You may then use AI tools to understand code structure, debug, and improve technical skills — but you must understand every line you submit, verify accuracy, and document your AI use in your weekly coding log. AI use is forbidden for historical analysis, interpretation, or writing. Always be the historian in the loop.
{{< /policy >}}
