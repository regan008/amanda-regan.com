About

I'm a historian of the late-nineteenth and twentieth-century United States and a specialist in digital and computational history — developing methods that uncover previously invisible patterns in the historical record.

I'm an Assistant Professor of History & Geography at Clemson University, where I serve as Director of Graduate Studies and help lead the first-of-its-kind Digital History Ph.D. Program. My courses train students to treat primary sources as data and to think computationally as a core historical skill.
