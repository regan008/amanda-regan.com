Historian · Digital & Computational

Amanda E. Regan

I develop methods that uncover previously invisible patterns in the historical record — combining large-scale data analysis, geospatial mapping, and algorithmic approaches with traditional archival research.

Assistant Professor at Clemson, Director of Graduate Studies, and a builder of the first-of-its-kind Digital History Ph.D. Program.

New book · Forthcoming Spring 2027

Shaping Up

Physical Fitness Initiatives for Women, 1880–1965

Traces how fitness became a mechanism for measuring and regulating women's bodies across nearly a century — never really about health, but an evolving standard for policing who counted as "fit" to participate in American life. University of Virginia Press.
