COURSE DESCRIPTION

This seminar introduces graduate students to the theory, methods, and historiography of digital history. We examine how computational tools — text analysis, mapping, network analysis, and data curation — reshape historical questions, and we read critically about the promises and limits of doing history with data. Students complete a digital project of their own design.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES
  • Situate digital history within broader historiographical debates.
  • Evaluate computational methods and their evidentiary claims.
  • Build and document a small but complete digital project.
  • Write critically about tools, data, and interpretation.
